HealthSync privacy

For the HealthSync iPhone app. Last updated November 23, 2026.

The short version: HealthSync reads workouts from Apple Health and sends them only to the servers you connect, only what you choose. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is ever sent to the app's developer.

What HealthSync reads

With your permission, HealthSync reads your workouts from Apple Health: the type, start and end, the device that recorded it, its GPS route, and its measurements (such as heart rate, pace, cadence, power, energy and steps). It only reads: it never writes to or changes anything in Apple Health. You choose what it may read in the Health app (your profile → Apps → HealthSync) and can change that at any time.

What stays on your iPhone

  • A list of your workouts and whether each was sent to each server.
  • The servers you connect: their name and address. Each server's key is kept in the iPhone's Keychain.
  • Files you export, in Files → On My iPhone → HealthSync, until you delete them.
  • A log of recent sync activity, used to show what happened and to help find problems.

This is kept out of iCloud backups. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your iPhone, and where it goes

  • To the servers you connect, and nowhere else. You add a server yourself, by scanning its setup code or entering its address. For each one you choose what it gets (everything, the route and a summary, or the route only), from which date, and which workout types. Workouts are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Each server's own privacy policy covers what it does with your workouts once it has them. HealthSync's developer doesn't run those servers.
  • Server logos: HealthSync asks each connected server's website for its icon, to show beside its name. That request goes only to the server's own address and carries no workout data.
  • Files you export go wherever you send them (AirDrop, Mail, Files); HealthSync doesn't send them anywhere itself.
  • Problem reports, only if you tap to send one: HealthSync prepares a report of its recent sync activity and settings, with server addresses and keys taken out, and you can read and edit it before you copy, share or post it. Nothing is sent automatically.

What HealthSync never does

  • No account, sign-in or email address.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services built into the app.
  • Your health data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never sent to the app's developer.

Notifications

If you allow them, HealthSync shows notifications from the iPhone itself, about sync progress and problems. They aren't sent through any server.

Your choices

  • Turn off a server's sending, or remove it, in HealthSync → Settings → Servers. Removing a server doesn't delete what it already received: ask that server.
  • Change what HealthSync may read in the Health app, or turn off its notifications in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything HealthSync kept on your iPhone.

Children

HealthSync isn't directed at children under 13.
