HealthSync

Send your Apple Health workouts, with their routes and heart rate, to servers you choose.

HealthSync reads workouts from Apple Health on your iPhone and sends them to the servers you connect, such as your running club's site or your own training log. It has no account, no ads and no tracking. Privacy policy.

Getting started

  1. Open HealthSync and allow it to read your workouts from Apple Health.
  2. Add a server: scan the setup code from the server's website (for example its phone-sync page), or enter its address. A setup code is for one phone and expires after a few minutes; ask the site for a new one if it does.
  3. Choose what that server gets, from which date, and which workout types. HealthSync sends them in the background from then on.

Common questions

  • A workout didn't arrive. Check that HealthSync may read workouts and routes (Health app → your profile → Apps → HealthSync), and that the server is set to receive that workout's type and date. Opening the app sends anything waiting.
  • How quickly do workouts send? iOS decides when apps may run in the background, so it's usually within minutes of a workout reaching Apple Health, sometimes longer. Opening HealthSync sends right away.
  • A server stopped accepting workouts. Its key may have been removed on the server's side. Get a new setup code from the server's website and scan it again.
  • Can I keep a copy myself? Yes: export workouts to Files → On My iPhone → HealthSync.
  • Who sees my workouts? Only the servers you connect. Each server's own privacy policy covers what it shows to others.

Contact
